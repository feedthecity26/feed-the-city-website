Huntsville, Alabama • Community Initiative

Opportunity Day Huntsville

A practical community event concept built around a simple goal: meet immediate needs, then connect people to the next opportunity.

Local organizer: Levon Harper Business: Total Finds Co LLC Status: Building partners & pilot event

What the project is

Opportunity Day Huntsville is being developed as a local service event that combines food distribution with access to employers, housing and recovery resources, résumé assistance, and introductions to legitimate entrepreneurship opportunities.

The project is designed to be measurable. Event goals, partner commitments, attendance, meals or supplies distributed, job-resource connections, and follow-up outcomes can be documented and published.

Immediate relief

Food, basic supplies, and practical assistance for people who need help now.

Opportunity

Employer connections, résumé help, housing and recovery resources, and income-pathway information.

Proof of impact

Photos, partner confirmation, attendance counts, distribution totals, and documented follow-up where participants consent.

Why a creator or sponsor would get involved

  • Concrete: a defined Huntsville event rather than a vague request for money.
  • Documentable: outcomes can be photographed, counted, and verified.
  • Expandable: a successful pilot can become a repeatable model.
  • Content-ready: the story centers on people receiving both immediate help and a next step.

Current ask

We are seeking established creators, businesses, nonprofits, employers, service providers, and sponsors willing to help design or support a pilot event in Huntsville. Support may be financial, in-kind, logistical, promotional, or direct participation.

No claim is made on this page that this initiative is a 501(c)(3) charity. Any charitable partnership or tax-deductible fundraising should be handled through an appropriately qualified nonprofit partner.

Verification & transparency

This public repository exists so potential partners can review the project as it develops. Major claims, partnerships, event dates, funding arrangements, and completed outcomes should be added here only when they can be documented.

Name clarification: This independent Huntsville initiative is not represented as affiliated with Tango Charities or its existing “Feed The City” program. The working public-facing name on this page is Opportunity Day Huntsville to reduce confusion.
